# Neta Capability Update Gate v1

Status: `CANONICAL`

Purpose: define when evidence is strong enough to change Neta after the falsification, decision-quality, Hebrew, or field programs.

## Default

**No capability expansion by intuition, literature volume, benchmark recurrence, or low score alone.**

A low capability score may mean `unmeasured`, not `bad`. The next action is therefore evidence acquisition unless a clean failure already exists.

## Promotion chain

Every proposed change must preserve this lineage:

`Evidence → Case → Failure Family → Hidden Judgment → Neighbor → Falsifiable Gate → Positive Control → Unseen HOLDOUT → Smallest Repair → Regression Check → Version`

Skip one link and the proposal remains case memory/research only.

## Required evidence for a core Neta change

A candidate must have all of:

1. **Trigger**
   - at least one clean Neta judgment failure; or
   - repeated surviving disadvantage versus baseline on the same decision family.

2. **Hidden judgment**
   - state exactly what distinction Neta failed to preserve.

3. **Neighboring non-fire case**
   - show a materially similar situation where the new rule must not fire.

4. **Falsifiable gate**
   - define observable conditions under which the rule is allowed to act.

5. **Deliberate positive control**
   - the pre-repair system must turn red on a constructed control that instantiates the failure.

6. **Smallest repair**
   - change the minimum prompt/contract/memory element needed to encode the missing judgment.
   - do not add a broad heuristic if a narrower discrimination suffices.

7. **Unseen HOLDOUT success**
   - the repaired system must succeed on new cases not used to design the repair.

8. **Neighbor regression check**
   - verify the repair does not break the non-fire case or a nearby canonical capability.

## Domain-specific additions

### Hebrew H1
A Hebrew-specific prompt change additionally requires:
- hidden H1 HOLDOUT evidence;
- no leakage of HOLDOUT gold before freeze;
- failure is semantic/pragmatic decision fidelity, not merely stylistic preference;
- faithful-English comparison where applicable.

### Hebrew H2
A reader-effect rule additionally requires:
- independent native-Hebrew human annotation;
- disagreement/ambiguity recorded;
- no collapse of expressed affect, speaker-state inference, and recipient effect.

### Targeted Falsification
A TF rule additionally requires success on both:
- the red target family;
- the green neighboring control.

### Decision Quality
A DQ-derived change requires a prospective adjudicated decision error, not only a retrospective story about what would have been better.

### FIELD
A FIELD-derived transfer rule requires at least one second context or a deliberately narrow context-bound wording. A single product field result may justify that product's decision without justifying a global Neta rule.

## Versioning rule

When a core change is finally earned:
- freeze the pre-change prompt blob SHA;
- create a new semantic Neta version;
- persist the exact diff and evidence lineage;
- never overwrite the historical baseline;
- start a new HOLDOUT wave for claims affected by the change.

## What does not count as evidence for promotion

- repeated mentions in the same research lineage;
- a synthetic TRAIN case created after the expected rule is known;
- higher subjective fluency;
- agreement from the owner alone on a FIELD-owned claim;
- a benchmark score with no case-level failure analysis;
- a low capability score whose evidence state is simply `NOT_YET_VALIDATED`.

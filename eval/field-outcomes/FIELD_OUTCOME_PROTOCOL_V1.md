# Neta Field Outcome Protocol v1

Status: `PREREGISTERED_NOT_YET_STARTED`

Purpose: test whether decisions that survive Neta's internal/owner/repository discrimination produce better outcomes with external users. This is the first protocol that can materially raise the `FIELD effectiveness` capability.

## Entry gate

A decision enters FIELD only after one of these is true:
- a narrow build has already been justified at the correct lower reality level and now makes an external-user claim;
- competing structurally valid variants remain and the unresolved question belongs to users;
- Neta explicitly stopped at FIELD because repository/owner evidence cannot answer notice/comprehension/preference/behavior.

Do not send unresolved implementation defects or owner-policy questions to FIELD just because users are available.

## Frozen pre-field record

Before observing external users, persist:
- case_id;
- product/domain;
- exact Neta version;
- decision that reached FIELD;
- material external-user claim;
- predicted observable outcome;
- smallest field discriminator;
- population/context boundary;
- success criterion;
- reversal criterion;
- any known risks of measurement reactivity.

## Outcome families — never composite

Choose only the family required by the claim:

### F1 Notice
Does the relevant element/state enter attention under realistic task conditions?

### F2 Comprehension
Can users state what happened/current state/next consequence without being taught the intended vocabulary?

### F3 Action discoverability
Can users identify the next useful action in the relevant mode?

### F4 Reliance/calibration
Where automated advice is involved, is reliance sensitive to correctness/evidence rather than merely increased subjective trust?

### F5 Behavioral task outcome
Does the change alter completion/error/recovery/decision quality under the actual task?

### F6 Preference / perceived craft
When the unresolved question is genuinely aesthetic/preferential, measure it as such rather than laundering it into usability.

### F7 Longitudinal accumulation
Does the interaction leave useful memory/progress/capability that changes later use?

## Minimum evidence per field claim

The smallest valid field observation depends on the claim. Default first pass:
- 5 independent participants for formative mechanism discovery where each observation is inspected qualitatively;
- larger samples only when a stable rate/estimate is itself the decision target;
- repeat across a second context/population when the intended claim generalizes beyond the first context.

The number 5 is not a universal usability-law threshold and does not establish prevalence. It is a practical minimum for a first qualitative discrimination pass; stop earlier only for severe obvious failure, continue when mechanisms remain heterogeneous.

## Comparison discipline

Where feasible, compare:
- current state/control;
- Neta-informed intervention;
- neighboring valid alternative when it tests the hidden judgment.

Change one material mechanism at a time when mechanism attribution matters. If multiple things change, adjudicate only the bundled intervention outcome, not the individual mechanism.

## Field invalidation conditions

Do not treat a field result as supporting Neta when:
- participant instructions reveal the intended answer;
- the measure is downstream of a probe that materially changes the target behavior without acknowledgement;
- the sample/context excludes the population to which the claim is generalized;
- implementation changed after the pre-field freeze;
- success criteria were rewritten after results appeared.

## First-wave target F1

Minimum viable field evidence:
- 6 prospective field cases;
- at least 3 different products/tasks;
- at least 2 different outcome families;
- at least 2 cases with a meaningful control/comparison;
- at least 1 case where field evidence reverses or materially narrows Neta's pre-field expectation;
- every case includes an explicit authority transition into FIELD.

## What this can and cannot prove

A successful field case can support:
- the specific product decision;
- the tested mechanism in the tested context;
- a candidate transfer rule if neighboring controls and later holdout support it.

It cannot by itself prove that Neta globally improves products.

This document applies the v0.2 assurance grammar **prospectively**.

## What `CANDIDATE_CAPABILITY` currently authorizes

For every Evidence Pass 1 claim currently at `CANDIDATE_CAPABILITY`, the shared v0.2 interpretation is:

### Allowed now

- use the distinction to design or refine an evaluation fixture;
- use it as a hypothesis/discriminator during analysis where the local evidence supports doing so;
- inspect whether the frozen v0.1 prompt already performs the judgment;
- specify a candidate prompt repair **only after** a clean-model baseline failure demonstrates the judgment is missing.

### Not authorized now

- editing `prompts/SYSTEM.md` merely because the research is strong;
- presenting the capability as a universal design law;
- asserting that Neta improves owner outcomes because the literature supports the underlying mechanism;
- asserting product-specific external-user effects without FIELD evidence;
- rewriting the original research status upward or downward under new v0.2 thresholds.

## Candidate-by-candidate assurance implications

### W1 — tacit → explicit
Research can support a mechanism/boundary about elicitation and reactivity. It does **not** establish that Neta improves design diagnosis or learning. Prompt use remains conditional on clean-model failure.

### W2 — perceptual hierarchy
Research can justify keeping quantity and perceptual organization separate and can support controlled discriminators. It does not authorize a universal low-density aesthetic or a claim that external users will choose the intended action.

### W3 — aesthetics / perceived craft
Owner aesthetic discomfort is legitimate owner evidence. Cross-context research can bound constructs. It does not make owner taste equal market preference or aesthetic-modernity equal usability.

### W4 — interaction feedback
Repo/environment evidence can separate completion latency, acknowledgement and state salience. Research may support mechanisms. Only FIELD can close claims about how strangers experience responsiveness.

### W5 — cognitive economy
Research can challenge “less is always better” and support separating intrinsic/extraneous cost from information value. Product-specific payoff/value remains local/field dependent.

### W6 — orientation/navigation
“One primary action” is conditional, not universal. Research can justify mode-sensitive hypotheses; the exact product state and field behavior still require local authorities.

### W7 — trust/uncertainty
Trust, reliance, correctness, evidence quality and action authority must remain separate. Research does not grant permission to optimize subjective trust as a target variable without the product's decision objective.

### W8 — adaptive instrumentation
Information gain alone does not authorize a probe. A probe also needs permission under measurement reactivity, cognitive cost, cheaper alternatives and protocol validity. This is directly relevant to proposed chess probes such as “which second move did you consider?”.

## Next evidence — not Source 29

The next legitimate evidence is **clean-model behavior**.

For each candidate family:

1. run the frozen v0.1 prompt in a clean context on the relevant fixture;
2. record baseline output before editing anything;
3. identify whether the failure is actually caused by missing hidden judgment;
4. if no failure appears, do not add a rule;
5. if a failure appears, specify the smallest prompt candidate and a neighbor at risk;
6. run the repair against both failure fixture and neighbor control;
7. only then consider `PROMPT_ELIGIBLE` → prompt change.

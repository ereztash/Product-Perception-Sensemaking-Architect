## C1 — Instrument existence is a separate state from evidence existence

**Type:** `MECHANISM` candidate

**Candidate wording:**

> A research system can have strong executable instrumentation while still having weak evidence memory because execution, scoring, deposition and claim-linkage are separate transitions.

**Evidence in import:** sophisticated instruments whose decisive output was stdout-only, temporary, overwritten, or never linked to a claim.

**Observed reality:** `R1` imported historical evidence.

**Required reality for a portfolio-general claim:** `RESEARCH` triangulation across independent research/software workflows, or a bounded wording limited to this portfolio after source-repo revalidation.

**Resolution authority:** `RESEARCH` for general mechanism; source `REPO` for historical/current instance.

**Requested use:** `DISCRIMINATE`

**Permission:** `ALLOW`

**Neighboring explanations:**

1. this is only ad-hoc script hygiene / research-code entropy;
2. this is mainly operator-capacity debt, not a distinct deposition mechanism;
3. this is one broader lifecycle failure where separating stages adds no decision value.

**Cheapest next discriminator:** reconstruct a small sample of instrument chains as explicit transitions:

```text
DESIGNED → LOCKED → RUN → SCORED → DEPOSITED → LINKED_TO_CLAIM → DECISION_EFFECT
```

Then test whether failures cluster at different transitions and imply different repairs.

**Reversal/narrowing:** if nearly all observed failures collapse to one transition (for example, never-run instruments), do not create multiple debt classes.

**Neta novelty state:** `POTENTIALLY_NEW`.

**Requested use:** `DISCRIMINATE`

**Neighboring explanations:**

1. scoring existed elsewhere but was not discovered by the extraction;

# 3. What appears genuinely additive after the Neta pass

The import does **not** currently justify a new Neta doctrine about instrument portfolios.

It creates three research distinctions worth further testing:

1. **Lifecycle-transition failure:** instrument existence and evidence existence may be separated by run, scoring, deposition and claim-linkage transitions.
2. **Closure debt:** research-design quality and run/closure capacity may be different bottlenecks and require different interventions.
3. **Output-vector integrity:** partial deposition of a deliberately multi-dimensional instrument may alter the decision the instrument was built to constrain.

A fourth item — **existence-search before build** — is an intervention candidate downstream of those distinctions, not yet an allowed rule.

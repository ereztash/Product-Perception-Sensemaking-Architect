## Important boundary case: NT-11 vs NT-10

Both are reusable prompts, but reuse alone is not enough.

### NT-10 — no material R&D
- lightweight visible scaffold;
- cheap and reversible;
- output structure rather than a strong claim about hidden reasoning topology;
- ordinary use itself reveals much of its utility.

### NT-11 — material R&D
- imposes a repeated reasoning/search topology;
- contains multiple unvalidated epistemic claims (`high-entropy anchors`, DAG/critical path as route to truth, distributional shifting, persona injection);
- cumulative use can systematically bias what evidence/arguments are considered;
- one successful output cannot validate the reusable policy.

This suggests that the consequential commitment can be **cumulative policy commitment**, not only a large one-time expenditure.

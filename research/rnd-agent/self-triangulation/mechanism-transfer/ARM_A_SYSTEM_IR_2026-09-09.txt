Build a Hebrew RTL web app called "Decision Trace — Plant Care".

TARGET DOMAIN
A person cares for several household plants. Before taking a meaningful care action such as watering, fertilizing, pruning, repotting, or moving a plant, they record what they observed, what they intend to do, and what response they expect. Days later they record what actually happened. The app is a decision-evidence journal, not a horticultural advice system. It must not claim that any action caused a plant outcome or that it improves plant health.

TELOS
Create a trustworthy longitudinal record of plant-care decisions so later patterns can be examined without changing or contaminating the evidence that produced them.

SYSTEM IR

STATE MACHINE
DECIDE -> COMMIT -> WAIT_RESULT -> REVEAL -> EXPLORE

INVARIANTS
I1. Measurement before interpretation: in DECIDE and COMMIT, show no historical pattern, prior success rate, recommendation, care tip, score, AI critique, or personalized weakness.
I2. Exactly one primary action per interaction state. Quiet secondary navigation is allowed.
I3. State determines what comes next. Screens render the state; they must not independently invent conflicting workflows.
I4. Missing recorded reasoning is missing data, not evidence that the person did not consider something.
I5. A pattern that predicts an outcome is not automatically specific to this plant or this person.
I6. A plant-specific residual is not automatically a causal mechanism.
I7. A plausible care intervention is not an established outcome until prospectively tested.
I8. Do not change what is measured and the intervention at the same time during a test.
I9. Every important validation rule needs a deliberate failing example on the same field/substrate and a valid negative control. A permanently green check is not evidence.
I10. App records may support observations about recorded decisions; only future observed outcomes can support an outcome claim, and observational history alone cannot establish causality.
I11. "No next recommendation" is a valid state. Never manufacture advice to fill the screen.

EPISTEMIC OBJECT
Any material finding must be representable with:
claim_id; statement; claim_kind = OBSERVATION | PREDICTION | MECHANISM | INTERVENTION | OUTCOME; resolution_authority = OWNER | APP_RECORD | RESEARCH | FIELD; required_reality; observed_reality; evidence_refs; state = SUPPORTED | PROVISIONAL | NOT_MEASURED | CONTRADICTED | INSUFFICIENT_REALITY; requested_use; permission = ALLOW | DEFER | DENY; reversal_condition.

TARGET-DOMAIN ENTITY AND RECORD
A decision record contains:
plant label; observed condition; action category; intended action; intended objective; expected observable response; confidence 0-100; optional alternatives considered; optional rationale; committed_at.
After commitment, the decision evidence is immutable.
A result contains: observed response category; optional note; observation date; logged_at.
A result observation date may not precede committed_at.

INTERACTION CONTRACT
Home resolves directly to the current state, not a feature dashboard.
DECIDE captures the decision record. One primary action: "מקבע החלטה".
COMMIT shows exactly what will be frozen and allows a quiet "חזרה לעריכה" that returns with all draft values preserved. One primary action: "מקבע לרשומה".
WAIT_RESULT shows only the frozen decision and one primary action: "יש תוצאה". No history or analysis.
RESULT LOGGING records observed response, optional note and date, with one save action.
REVEAL may only appear after a result exists. It separates "תצפית" from "פרשנות", compares expected and observed response descriptively, does not provide causal explanation, and says NOT_MEASURED when evidence is insufficient.
Closing REVEAL must transition the core machine to EXPLORE.
EXPLORE shows the longitudinal record and bounded descriptive summaries with epistemic status. It may offer one clear primary action to begin a new DECIDE cycle.
Include a quiet Claims surface showing at least four claim objects with distinct states and permissions, including a FIELD-required intervention/outcome claim that is DEFER or DENY.
Include a quiet internal Validation surface. Seed at least eight historical records. Include one deliberately malformed fixture whose observation date precedes commitment and/or another exact-domain defect. The validator must fail the malformed fixture on the same actual field path used by the UI and pass a valid control.

ANTI-PATTERNS
Do not show analytics while a decision is being recorded.
Do not rewrite or critique the user's care action before commitment.
Do not treat an empty alternatives field as proof no alternative was considered.
Do not label a retrospective correlation as a personal or plant-specific causal mechanism.
Do not claim improvement from retrospective data.
Do not make a side route pretend to be a state transition.
Do not validate a surrogate timestamp when the actual user-entered observation date is the field that matters.

IMPLEMENTATION BOUNDARY
Reversible prototype only. No external AI API, auth, or backend required. Local/demo persistence is enough. Hebrew RTL, desktop-first responsive, quiet research-journal feel, generous whitespace, minimal chrome. Build a working flow, not a mockup. Do not add features beyond this contract.
